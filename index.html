🌊 SEND friendly

Swimming lessons for ages 4 and up

From first strokes to confident lengths, Swim Start welcomes complete beginners and improvers aged 4 and over — children, teenagers and adults alike. Our friendly, fully certified team teaches at a pace that suits each swimmer.

Everyone is welcome here

Swim Start is a SEND-friendly swimming school. We adapt our teaching to every swimmer's needs, keep group sizes comfortable, and take the time to build trust in the water. If your child or family member needs particular support, just tell us — we would love to hear from you before your first session so we can get things right.
