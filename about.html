About us

A little about Swim Start and how we teach.

A team that loves teaching

Swim Start was built around a simple idea: everyone deserves to feel safe and confident in the water. We are a swimming school for ages 4 and up, and we are proud to be SEND friendly — adapting how we teach so that every swimmer gets what they need.

Between our teachers, lifeguards and swimming assistants there are bundles of teaching experience, and every member of the team is fully certified. Whether you are arranging a first lesson for a nervous beginner or looking to polish your technique, you are in good hands.
